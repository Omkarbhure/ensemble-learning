Supported Use Cases & Input Validation Rules
The ensemble is strictly calibrated for single MNIST-style handwritten digits.
✅ Supported Inputs
  • Single Digits (0 to 9): Drawn clearly in the canvas.
  • Scanned/Uploaded Digits: Clean PNG, JPG, or JPEG images.
  • Centered Single Strokes: Clear handwriting with reasonable stroke width.
🚫 Rejected Inputs
  • Alphabets & Letters (e.g. A, B, C, X, Y, Z).
  • Multi-digit Numbers (e.g. 10, 72, 999).
  • Doodles, Symbols & Shapes (e.g. *, #, @, stars, faces).
  • Complex Photos & Backgrounds (photos, icons, clipart).
